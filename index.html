Minje Park

B.S. student in Physics and Electrical Engineering, Sungkyunkwan University

Interested in cosmology, astrophysics, and machine learning.
Currently focusing on diffusion, flow matching, and Transformer models for cosmological simulations.

Email CV Scholar GitHub LinkedIn

Research experience

  1. Generative AI for Cosmological Simulation and Super-Resolution

    • Developing generative AI models for cosmological simulation and super-resolution.

  2. Transformer-Based Reconstruction Model for SWGO

    • Investigated position embedding strategies for a Transformer-based primary-particle reconstruction model in SWGO.
    • Developed flow matching models to synthesize SWGO detector signals from simulation data.

  3. Generative AI for IceCube and SWGO

    • Developed diffusion models and Mixture Density Networks to synthesize PMT signals for the SWGO tank simulation.
    • Built flow and diffusion models to generate neutrino events in the IceCube Observatory.

  4. CNN and Vision Transformer for IceCube Camera Analysis

    • Developed CNN and Vision Transformer models for the IceCube Upgrade Camera System.
    • Results presented as a poster and proceedings paper at ICRC 2025 (arXiv:2507.18525).
